# Ranking Guide

Ranking changes order only. It must never change nutrition.

Deterministic evidence includes:

- Exact normalized identity
- Brand and restaurant agreement
- Barcode equality
- Product form and flavor
- Serving and unit compatibility
- Source completeness and quality
- Verified catalog authority
- Confirmed favorites and recency
- Prior source selection
- Contradictory terms and implausible servings

User learning raises previously confirmed identities and servings within the same identity tier. It does not rewrite calories or macros.

When duplicate identities materially disagree, MacroMesh selects one complete record by source strength and marks it for review. It never averages or mixes provider fields.
